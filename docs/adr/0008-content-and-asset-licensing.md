# ADR-0008 — Content and asset licensing: CC BY 4.0

Status: active
Date: 2026-09-13

## Context

- [VOICE] (2026-09-13) The owner first proposed **CC BY-NC-ND 3.0** for "all
  non-synthesized audio clips and assets", then revised to **CC BY 4.0** — "in
  the spirit of MIT license, I drop NC and ND."
- [DECISION] ADR-0002: the repository's own **code** is MIT.
- [DECISION] ADR-0003: the third-party licence boundary (permissive preferred;
  copyleft only over process/network boundaries).
- [FACT] The repository currently commits **no** audio or media assets — audio is
  gitignored and recordings are never committed (ADR-0006). This is therefore a
  **forward-looking declaration**, not a relicence of anything present.

## Decision

- [DECISION] **Code** stays **MIT** (ADR-0002).
- [DECISION] **Content and assets** authored for this repository — the
  documentation, and any non-synthesized audio clips or other assets we ship —
  are licensed **CC BY 4.0**
  (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>): reuse freely, with
  attribution.
- [DECISION] **Synthesized** test audio is generated by the tool at run time and
  is not a shipped asset; it needs no separate licence.
- Third-party material keeps its own licence and is never bundled (ADR-0003 /
  [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)).
- The tool's **outputs** — transcripts and records produced from a user's own
  audio — belong to the **user**, not to this project.

## Rationale

- **Uniformly permissive.** MIT (code) and CC BY (content) both mean "use it
  freely, credit the source" — consistent for a clean-room OSS project, and it
  avoids a two-tier licence (permissive code / restrictive media) that is hard to
  explain.
- **NC/ND dropped deliberately.** NonCommercial and NoDerivatives would make the
  assets *less* reusable than the code they accompany.
- **CC BY for content, not code.** Creative Commons advises against CC licences
  for software, which is why this applies only to content and assets; CC BY is
  the standard permissive content licence, the natural counterpart to MIT.

## Discarded alternatives

- **CC BY-NC-ND 3.0/4.0** (the owner's first proposal) — restricts commercial use
  and modification; inconsistent with the permissive code.
- **No separate content licence** (assets inherit MIT) — MIT is a *software*
  licence and a poor fit for prose/audio.

## Consequences / review hook

- Applies **only** to assets we author; it cannot licence third-party content.
- Revisit if a shipped asset is derived from something incompatible with CC BY.
